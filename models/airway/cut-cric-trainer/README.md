# C.U.T. Cricothyrotomy Trainer

Mirror of the **Cricothyrotomy Unlimited Trainer (C.U.T.)** by SpragClutch, released into the
public domain (CC0 1.0) on Printables. Because the license is CC0, the complete model is
hosted directly in this repository — no external account or link needed to get the files.

Original posting: https://www.printables.com/model/698420-cricothyrotomy-cric-crico-crioid-trainer

## Why it's here

The designer built it for hospitals and medical facilities that burn through hundreds of
expensive commercial replacement skins a year keeping staff proficient. The goals were
affordable, anatomically effective, and durable across hundreds of uses — the same goals
this repository has for austere and resistance-medicine training.

## Parts

| File | What it is | Print |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `neck_unit.stl` | Full anatomical neck and airway with realistic landmarks | PLA, no supports |
| `cartilage_overlay.stl` | Flexible cricothyroid cartilage overlay | TPU (NinjaTek Cheetah recommended), supports on |
| `tool_tray.stl` | Parts tray for consumables and tools | PLA, no supports |
| `build_guide.pdf` | Designer's assembly guide and part sources | — |

**Settings:** 0.3 mm layer height, 16 % or greater infill.

## Consumables

Tape or a commercial replacement skin layered over the cartilage overlay serves as the
membrane that gets cut. The build guide lists the designer's part sources.
