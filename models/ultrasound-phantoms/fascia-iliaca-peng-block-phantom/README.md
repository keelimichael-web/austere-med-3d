# Fascia iliaca / PENG block ultrasound phantom

DIY ultrasound-guided nerve block phantom built around a 3D-printed hemipelvis, developed and tested with residents in October 2026 and shared with the Med Annex working group. Full write-up with print files is pending as a book appendix; this page preserves the build so it can be reproduced now.

## Bill of materials

- 3D-printed hemipelvis (anterior half only; cut the model, the posterior half is not needed under the probe)
- Ballistic gelatin: per batch, 100 g gelatin + 900 mL water + 20 g flour (flour or cornstarch gives tissue-like echogenicity; ratios from published simulation literature)
- 550 cord strands for nerve bundles
- Surgical tubing for vessels, filled with water, ends sealed with printed plugs (hot glue will not bond inside the tubing)
- Plastic wrap (Saran) for fascial planes
- A basin to cast in

## Build sequence

1. Print the hemipelvis, place in the basin, and pour gelatin to half depth.
2. Lay 550 cord strands as nerve bundles. Free them from the first gelatin layer once it sets, so they move when anesthetic is injected beneath them.
3. Place water-filled surgical tubing as vessels, plugged at both ends with printed plugs.
4. Lay three layers of plastic wrap across the model as the fascia iliaca, then pour the next gelatin layer.
5. Lay three more layers of wrap as the fascia lata, then pour the final layer.

## Observed performance

Worked:
- Residents got a reasonable ultrasound view of AIIS, superior pubic ramus, fascial layers, and vessels.
- PLA reads echodense, as bone should.
- The wrap layers dissected realistically under injection, showing anesthetic spread along the fascial plane.

Needs work:
- Tactile feedback was not fully realistic; try a lower gelatin density.
- Fascial layers need to be thicker to give a felt "pop" on puncture (the blind version of this block depended on that feel), while staying flexible enough to conform to the model surface.
- Surgical tubing is more echoreflective than real vessels, filled or not. Good enough as placeholders; other tubing types worth trying.
