# In memory of Ian Daniel

This repository is dedicated to Ian Daniel.

Ian was a nurse practitioner working toward board certification in emergency medicine, and he was within a few credits of earning it. His goal was simple and serious: to become the kind of emergency provider who could bring real care to people in hard places, with whatever was on hand.

That is the same provider this collection exists to train. Every model added here helps someone practice the work Ian was preparing his whole career to do.

The collection stays open, and it keeps growing in his name.
